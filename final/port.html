My father, and by extension I, have been raising fish for a while but only as a hobby. Through him I have learned how to care for a tank and all the maintenance it requires as well as other things such as recognizing and preventing tank borne diseases. A common disease is called Ich which is most identifiable by what looks I can only describe as the eye gunk you get around your eyes after waking up. However my father is retired but has the unfortunate problem of not being able to sit down, so he's gone further with his hobby. In our garage where he keeps his new fish breeding business he calls "Aztec Tropical Fish" (although it's still a work in process). While he's had set backs he always bounces back and is selling to local fish stores the guppies which he specializes in. Though in the future he hopes to have a functioning website and everything that comes with a real business.
