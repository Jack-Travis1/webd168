The Red Velvet Swordtails are species of fish with sexual dimorphism where the males are sleeker and possesses a long and black bottom tail fin (hence the name swordtail) while the females do not. Some of them can be pretty aggressive so my father isn't the biggest fan but they can make great additions to a tank.

Mystery Snails
Mystery snails are my personal favorite of a tank and can be quite the unsung hero. Snails in general should always be a part of any fish tank just based on how much of an impact they make on keeping a tank healthy. Mystery snails come in different colors which keeps on the mystery of what species they are.
My father has started breeding many kinds of guppies such as Blue Nebulas and Red Moscows. These are by far my father's favorite fish and it's not just because of how much money he can make selling them. Each Moscow is its own pretty penny and so are a major investment for your tank when buying from a store.
